# Governance Model

## Overview
This document outlines the governance structure for the **Book Library** project. It clarifies roles, decision-making processes, and how the community works together.

## Roles & Responsibilities

### Maintainers
Maintainers are responsible for the strategic direction of the project.
- **Responsibilities**:
  - Reviewing and merging Pull Requests.
  - Triage and management of Issues.
  - Releasing new versions.
  - Enforcing the Code of Conduct.
- **Current Maintainers**:
  - [Name/Handle]

### Contributors
Anyone who submits a Pull Request, reports an issue, or participates in discussions.
- **Responsibilities**:
  - Follow the [Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).
  - Follow the [Contributing Guide](CONTRIBUTING.md).

## Decision Making
- **Consensus**: We aim for consensus among maintainers for major decisions.
- **Lazy Consensus**: If no objections are raised within 72 hours of a proposal, it is considered accepted.
- **Tie-Breaking**: In the event of a deadlock, the Project Lead has the final say.

## Project Ownership
This project is owned by the core maintainers. In the event a maintainer steps down, they may nominate a successor.

## Amendments
This governance model may be amended by a vote of the maintainers.
